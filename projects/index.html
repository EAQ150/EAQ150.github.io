Executable Disc Image (EDI)

EAQ150

EDI, or Executable Disc Image is meant to be a competitor to AppImage by combining the capabilities of an ISO file and an Appimage while optimizing them, so they can able to run with different flags for performance tweaks. It aims to stay under a total of 100KB, that way it shouldn't take up too much space on your computer.
