Executable Disc Image (EDI)

EDI, or Executable Disc Image is meant to be a competitor to AppImage by combining the capabilities of an ISO file and an Appimage, with being able to run them with different flags. It aims to stay under a total of 100KB, that way it shouldn't take up too much space on your computer.
